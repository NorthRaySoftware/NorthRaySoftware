# NorthRay suite roadmap (from CJ's answers, 14 November 2026)

Ten questions per app; these are the decisions. "All" means every listed priority area is wanted.

## NorthRay Launcher
Done in 0.19.0 (14 November 2026): all ten. Also, at CJ's request, every app now draws minimise, maximise and close in its own top area.

1. Updates install **automatically** in the background when the app isn't open.
2. Start with Windows: **off, with a setting**.
3. Library: **big app cards** (artwork, version, Open).
4. Extras: **tray icon with quick-launch**.
5. First run: **short 3-step welcome tour**.
6. (Removed: a section for another creator's apps.)
7. Install location: **default per-user folder, changeable in Settings**.
8. Themes: **Light, Dark, follow Windows**.
9. Downloads **checked against the catalogue checksum** before installing.
10. Priority: **all** (auto-updates + tray, look and feel, install location + safety).

## NorthRay Writing Studio
Stage 1 done in 0.20.0: focus/typewriter mode, font and page width (9), spell check switch and first style hints (2; better hints and custom rules done in 0.21.0, rules kept per world or per independent book), linked names (4, in the text), backups every 30 minutes and to a chosen folder (6). Version history per chapter already existed (Review > Changes, and the details panel).
Stage 3 done in 0.23.0: image boards (5) and linked wiki pages in Worldbuilding (4); the Studio's own spell checker with per-world dictionaries (2).
Stage 2 done in 0.22.0: timeline, corkboard and story-structure templates (8). Also: names link by their parts, shared names link to everyone who has them, themed menus, name cards.

1. Used for **novels/series and comics/scripts equally**.
2. Next tools: **focus/typewriter mode, version history per chapter, spell check + style hints**.
3. Exports: **EPUB (improve styling and covers), print-ready PDF, comic script format**.
4. World bible: **linked wiki pages** (names in the text link to their page).
5. Art: **image boards** (character sheets, covers).
6. Backup: **local auto-backup plus a folder you pick** (e.g. OneDrive).
7. Review: **share a review copy** (export for a beta reader, import their comments).
8. Plotting: **timeline view, corkboard/index cards, story-structure templates**.
9. Editor: **font and page-width choices**.
10. Priority: **all**.

## Writing Studio: World bible, round 2 (CJ's answers, 14 November 2026)

Looking at CJ's library (81 entries: 66 characters, 9 places, 3 creatures, 3 races):
- **Duplicates.** 14 copies of Zah’mes, made within two seconds of each other (a bug). Eleven entries appear twice, once in Alvora and once left over from Shards of Light, which was an independent book until it joined the world.
- **Entries under the wrong kind.** Demon, Fluxians and Magica are filed as characters. Bluebolt, Lamentis and Elandroth each appear under two kinds.
- **Almost no content.** Only 8 entries have any details, and those details are facets; only 4 relationships are filled in.

Decisions:
1. Duplicates: **merge automatically**, keeping every name, detail, picture and link.
2. Wrong kinds: **both**, meaning the Studio suggests a kind for CJ to confirm, and any entry has an easy "Change kind".
3. Book joins a world: **its entries move into the world's bible**, merging any with the same name.
4. New entries: **the Studio offers new names as you write**, **quick add with a template**, and **right-click a name in Writing**.
5. Character pages: **appearance and personality, backstory and goals, abilities and powers, age/birth/status**.
6. Layout: **a template per kind that CJ can edit** (sections renamed, added or removed, per world).
7. Pictures: **a gallery per entry**, with image boards made inside the entry rather than separately.
8. Relationships: **a family tree**.
9. Over time: **notes per book**, shown for the book being written.
10. Appears in: **chapters, with counts**.
11. Continuity checks: **spellings of names, unused entries, facts that disagree** (bible against text).
12. Places: **both the place tree and maps with pins**.
13. Lore: **a glossary with pronunciation**, plus **magic/power rules** pages.
14. Sharing: **a wiki website, a printable series bible (PDF), and a reader's guide**.
15. Spoilers: **per fact**, marked by the book where it's revealed.
16. Order: **all of it, in this order**: clean-up, templates and galleries, family tree, maps, checks, sharing.
17. Creature trees: CJ says they're a mess and need to be redone. This goes with the clean-up.
    - **Species › breed › individuals** (Dragon › Wyvern › Bluebolt). Named individuals are marked as such, not as kinds.
    - A named creature is **one entry that is both**: a Character, and listed under its breed in Creatures.
    - Shown as an **indented list**, opening and closing like folders.
    - Built by **dragging onto a parent**, a **"Kind of" field** on the page, and **"+ Add a kind"** on a species.
