About

CJ Radio is CJ Philip's own radio app and the home of The CJ Philip Show: conversations, guests, listener questions and the occasional special. Saturday is the regular broadcast day, with extra shows announced now and then. When nothing is on, you can still check the schedule and catch up on recordings.

The CJ Philip Show

CJ Radio is CJ Philip's own station and the home of The CJ Philip Show, hosted by author and broadcaster CJ Philip, usually on Saturdays, with occasional extra broadcasts.

Schedule

Programme times are published in a calendar, and the CJ Radio app reads the same one. Add it to Google Calendar, or subscribe from Outlook, Apple Calendar or another app with this calendar link.

How NorthRay Software and CJ Philip are connected

Call-ins happen on a Discord Stage during live shows. Callers must be 16 or over, and calls are broadcast and recorded.

Features

  • Listen live through the Twitch player, with a sound-only option
  • The schedule of confirmed shows, in your own time zone
  • Recordings to catch up on
  • Call in on Discord during live shows (16 and over)
  • CJ's own tools for running the show, on his PC
