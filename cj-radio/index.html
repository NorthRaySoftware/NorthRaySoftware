About

CJ Radio is where CJ Philip hosts live talk programmes for the community: conversations, guests, listener questions and the occasional special. Saturday is the regular broadcast day, with extra shows announced now and then. When nothing is on, you can still check the schedule and catch up on recordings.

The CJ Philip Show

CJ Radio's first programme is The CJ Philip Show, hosted by author and broadcaster CJ Philip, usually on Saturdays, with occasional extra broadcasts. The app is built to carry other presenters and programmes as they join.

TwitchYouTubeDiscord

Schedule

Programme times are published in a calendar, and the CJ Radio app reads the same one. Add it to Google Calendar, or subscribe from Outlook, Apple Calendar or another app with this calendar link.

Call-ins happen on a Discord Stage during live shows. Callers must be 16 or over, and calls are broadcast and recorded.

Features

  • Listen live through the Twitch player, with a sound-only option
  • Schedule of confirmed programmes in your own time zone
  • Recordings to catch up on
  • Call in on Discord during live shows (16 and over)
  • Presenter tools for running a show
