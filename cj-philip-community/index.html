About

CJ Philip Community is the home of the apps associated with CJ Philip, starting with CJ Radio. It installs them, keeps them up to date and opens them in one click, and new CJ apps appear on their own as they're released. NorthRay Launcher installs CJ Philip Community itself; the CJ apps are got and opened from here. It's an associated creator project published by NorthRay Software.

The home of the CJ Philip apps, starting with CJ Radio: they're installed, updated and opened from here. NorthRay Launcher installs CJ Philip Community itself. How NorthRay Software and CJ Philip are connected.

Features

  • Installs, updates, opens and uninstalls CJ Radio and the other CJ Philip apps
  • Shows what's installed, running, or has an update
  • Downloads are checked against their published size and SHA-256 before they install
  • New CJ apps appear on their own as they're released
  • Light, dark, or matching Windows

What's new

Version 0.16.2 · 2026-11-13

  • Fixed: CJ Philip Community closed straight away on PCs where an earlier version had saved its theme setting. It now reads the old and new forms.
  • If it ever does hit an error it can't recover from, it says what went wrong and saves a short report, instead of closing without a word.

Version 0.16.1 · 2026-11-13

  • CJ Philip Community is now the home of the CJ Philip apps: it installs, updates, opens and uninstalls CJ Radio itself, using the same catalogue and download checks as NorthRay Launcher.
  • An installed app always opens from its card. Before, an available update turned the card into a link to NorthRay Launcher instead of opening the app.
  • A progress bar and Cancel while an app downloads and installs; Check for updates on the page.
