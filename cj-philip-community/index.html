About

CJ Philip Community is a small launcher for the apps associated with CJ Philip, starting with CJ Radio. It shows which are installed, running or have an update, and opens them in one click. Anything not installed yet is fetched through NorthRay Launcher, and new CJ apps appear on their own as they're released. It's an associated creator project published by NorthRay Software. Nothing else needs it: CJ Radio installs and runs without it.

Optional. It's a small launcher for the CJ Philip apps, starting with CJ Radio, which runs fine without it. It doesn't install anything itself: NorthRay Launcher does that. How NorthRay Software and CJ Philip are connected.

Features

  • Opens CJ Radio and the other CJ Philip apps in one click
  • Shows what's installed, running, or has an update
  • Gets anything missing through NorthRay Launcher
  • New CJ apps appear on their own as they're released
  • Light, dark, or matching Windows
