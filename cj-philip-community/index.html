About

CJ Philip Community is an optional app centred on author and broadcaster CJ Philip: what's on CJ Radio, announcements, creative updates and community events, with links to the NorthRay apps that go with them. It's an associated creator project published by NorthRay Software. Nothing else needs it: CJ Radio and the Writing Studio install and run without it.

Optional. CJ Radio and the Writing Studio don't need it, and it doesn't install anything itself: NorthRay Launcher does that.

Features

  • What's on CJ Radio and when, in your own time zone
  • Announcements and creative updates
  • Community events
  • Links to the NorthRay apps that go with it
  • Planned: friends, messages and notifications, once accounts exist
