About

NorthRay Browser is a desktop web browser for Windows. Web pages are shown by Microsoft Edge WebView2, the Chromium engine that comes with Windows 11 and is kept up to date by Windows, with its sandbox left fully on. It has tabs; an address bar that opens addresses, local files and searches (and never sends a file path or a local network address to a search engine); a start page with your bookmarks; bookmarks with folders, a bookmarks bar and a bookmarks page, imported from or exported to Chrome, Edge and Firefox; and saved passwords and form filling, kept encrypted on your PC by the engine's password manager. Certificate errors are always refused. History, a downloads panel, profiles, private windows and the creative tools (collections, notes, boards and more) come in later versions.

Features

  • Tabs: open, close, reopen closed tabs, switch with the keyboard; links that open new windows open as tabs
  • Address bar: web addresses, local files and searches with DuckDuckGo, Google, Bing or another engine; file paths and local network addresses are never searched for
  • Bookmarks: a star in the address bar, a bookmarks bar, folders, a bookmarks page with search, and import and export in the format Chrome, Edge and Firefox use; a daily backup
  • A start page with a greeting, a search field, your bookmarks as tiles and recently closed tabs; nothing on it loads from the internet
  • Saved passwords and form filling, offered after you sign in, encrypted with a key protected by your Windows account; turn them off or delete them all from the menu
  • A padlock for secure sites and a plain "Not secure" for others; certificate problems are refused with an explanation
  • Full screen, printing, developer tools, and light and dark themes that follow Windows
  • Edge's sandbox and SmartScreen protection against phishing and harmful downloads; no telemetry of NorthRay's own
  • Back up bookmarks, reference boards, notes, settings and history to one file, and restore it here or on another PC

What's new

Version 0.9.0 · 2026-11-18

  • Drag a tab down out of the tab strip to give it a window of its own.
  • The tabs you had open come back when the browser starts. Their pages open as you go to each tab, so starting stays quick.
  • Closing a window with five or more tabs asks first.
  • Each site remembers its own zoom (Ctrl + mouse wheel, or Ctrl and plus or minus). Ctrl+0 puts it back to 100%.
  • Tabs playing sound show a speaker. Click it to mute the tab, or use Mute tab in the tab's right-click menu.
  • Rest the pointer on a tab to see its title, its site and a small picture of the page.
  • Turn the mouse wheel over the tabs to move between them. The tab menu has “Close tabs to the right”, and the tabs' close buttons are bigger and easier to hit.
  • The address bar shows the site's name clearly with the rest of the address fainter, has a Copy link button, and its right-click menu has Paste and go.
  • Bookmarks pick up their site's icon once you've visited the site, including bookmarks imported from another browser.
  • No white flash before a page appears in a dark window, and scrolling glides smoothly.
  • Reference boards remember the board you were on, and each board's size of pictures (Ctrl + mouse wheel) and where you'd scrolled to.

Version 0.8.1 · 2026-11-14

  • Fixed: buttons in question boxes didn't respond to clicks when Windows' display scaling was above 100%.

Version 0.8.0 · 2026-11-14

  • Back up and restore (in the menu): one file holding your bookmarks, reference boards and their pictures, start page notes and background, settings, and (if you choose) your history. Keep it, or take it to another PC: no account, nothing leaves your computer unless you move the file.
  • Restoring checks the backup first, then restarts the browser to put it in place. Whatever it replaces is kept in a “Before restore” folder, so nothing is lost.
  • Saved passwords and sign-ins aren't in backups: they stay in the engine's protected store on this PC. The downloads list isn't either, as it points at files on this PC.
  • Long menus show whole again when they fit on the screen; only very long lists scroll.

Version 0.7.2 · 2026-11-14

  • The video pop-out takes the shape of the video, and keeps it as you resize it.
  • Controls on the pop-out while the mouse is over it: back 10 seconds, play or pause, forward 10 seconds.
  • “Skip ad” appears on the pop-out whenever the site offers to skip an advert (YouTube's button, or a “Skip ad” on other sites), even when the mouse isn't over it, and presses the site's own button for you.
