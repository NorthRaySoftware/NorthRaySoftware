About NorthRay Software

NorthRay Software is an independent software project making creative applications for writing, publishing and broadcasting.

It's built around a simple belief: good software should be accessible to everyone, not just those who can afford it. Financial circumstances shouldn't decide whether someone can create, learn or express themselves.

So the aim is useful, dependable desktop software that people genuinely enjoy using: thoughtfully designed, reliable, and capable of standing alongside established commercial alternatives, without unnecessary complications, artificial restrictions or expensive subscriptions.

We would rather release one application we're proud of than ten that barely work.

The current applications are NorthRay Writing Studio, a writing and publishing studio for authors, and CJ Radio, a broadcasting app. NorthRay Launcher installs and updates them.

Free by philosophy

Our aim is to make NorthRay applications free to use, without subscriptions, paywalls or deliberately restricted features. If a feature makes the software better, the intention is for everyone to benefit from it.

NorthRay Software isn't built around investors or growth targets. Development, distribution and maintenance do have real costs, so we'll explore sustainable ways of supporting the project, without compromising the accessibility of the core applications, and explain them openly.

Your work belongs to you

Wherever practical, the apps keep your work on your own computer, work offline, and import and export established formats. Your writing, artwork and projects belong to you, and you shouldn't feel trapped inside an app or dependent on a constant internet connection.

Growing at our own pace

Software development is an ongoing process and mistakes are inevitable; what matters is acknowledging them and improving. Feedback, ideas and constructive criticism are welcome: the people who use the software should help shape its future. We're starting small, and we're comfortable with that.

NorthRay Software and CJ Philip

Two separate identities, closely associated. NorthRay Software makes the software; CJ Philip is a creative partner whose work some of the apps are built around.

NorthRay Software

The name the applications are developed and published under. It's responsible for the software: the apps themselves, their updates, NorthRay Launcher, this website and support.

CJ Philip

A Scottish author, artist and broadcaster, and the host of The CJ Philip Show. He lends his name and creative brand to CJ Radio and CJ Philip Community; his books, artwork and programmes are his own.

Where they meet

  • CJ Radio is a broadcasting app whose first programme is The CJ Philip Show. It's built to carry other presenters and programmes as well.
  • CJ Philip Community is an optional launcher for the CJ Philip apps, starting with CJ Radio. None of the other apps need it.
  • NorthRay Writing Studio is for any author, whatever they write.

The origin story

It began with a writing app. NorthRay Software had been building NorthRay Writing Studio, a place for authors to keep their worlds, series and books together, from the first idea through to a finished edition.

Then NorthRay Software approached CJ Philip, an emerging author and broadcaster, with an offer: a radio app built to carry The CJ Philip Show.

Before saying yes, CJ Philip asked to try the writing app. He spent time with the Writing Studio and liked what he found, and that settled it. He agreed to the radio app, CJ Radio, and to lend his creative brand to the project.

That collaboration became the foundation of CJ Philip Community, where the applications come together under a shared community identity.

Questions about the apps go to NorthRay Software through the contact page. For CJ Philip's books, art and shows, follow his own channels, such as the links on the CJ Radio page.
