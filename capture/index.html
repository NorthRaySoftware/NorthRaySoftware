About

NorthRay Capture is a livestreaming and recording app for Windows, being built in stages towards a real alternative to OBS Studio: per-application audio (stream a game and your microphone while music and voice chat stay private), streaming to several platforms at once from one encode, and staying light on older PCs. This version captures pictures: a whole display, a single window (found again when the program restarts) or a camera or capture card, composed on the graphics card into a live preview. You can move, resize, crop, rotate, mirror, hide and lock each source, and arrange them in scenes. It doesn't capture sound, record or stream yet: each of those is labelled in the app with the stage it arrives in, starting with sound next.

Features

  • Capture a whole display (Desktop Duplication, with Windows Graphics Capture and a compatibility mode as fallbacks), with or without the mouse pointer
  • Capture one window, even when it's covered (Windows Graphics Capture); it's found again when the program restarts
  • Webcams and capture cards that Windows sees as cameras
  • A live preview: drag to move, drag the handles to resize (Shift for any shape), Alt+drag to crop, with snapping to the canvas edges and centre (hold Ctrl to stop it)
  • Fit, stretch, centre, rotate, mirror, reset; hide, lock and reorder sources; the arrow keys nudge
  • Scenes: add, rename, duplicate, reorder and delete, switch with Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9; a source can be shared by several scenes and is captured once
  • The workspace: resize, rearrange, hide and save the panels as layouts
  • Light on the computer: when the preview is hidden and nothing is recording or streaming, nothing is composed
  • Settings: theme, low-resource mode, preview and output frame rates, canvas size (1080p, 720p, 1440p or vertical 9:16); the encoders your PC offers
  • Not yet: sound, recording and streaming (each arrives in a later version)

What's new

Version 0.2.0 · 2026-11-14

  • Video capture: add a whole display, a window or a camera or capture card from + in Sources, and see it live in the preview.
  • Displays are captured with Desktop Duplication, or Windows Graphics Capture or a slower compatibility mode where that isn't available; you can choose the method and whether to show the mouse pointer from a source's right-click menu.
  • Windows are captured with Windows Graphics Capture (they can be covered by other windows). Capture remembers the program and finds its window again when it restarts; minimised windows keep their last picture.
  • Arrange sources on the preview: drag to move, drag the handles to resize (Shift for any shape), Alt+drag a handle to crop, with snapping to the edges and centre of the canvas (hold Ctrl to place freely). The arrow keys nudge (Shift for 10 pixels).
  • Each source's right-click menu: rename, hide, lock, fit to the canvas (Ctrl+F), stretch (Ctrl+S), centre (Ctrl+D), reset (Ctrl+R), rotate, flip, remove the crop, and order. The eye and lock buttons in Sources hide and lock.
  • A source can be shown in several scenes (“A source from another scene” under +) and is captured once.
  • The status bar shows how long the graphics card takes for each frame, and the preview shows the frame rate being kept.
  • Not yet: sound, recording and streaming.

Version 0.1.0 · 2026-11-14

  • First version, in early development: the foundation. It doesn't capture, record or stream yet.
  • The workspace: a preview area and Scenes, Sources, Audio mixer and Controls panels. Drag the dividers to resize, drag a panel's header (or right-click it) to move or hide it, and save, load or reset layouts from Layout in the top bar.
  • Scenes: add, rename, duplicate, reorder and delete, switch with Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 or the arrow keys; saved automatically.
  • Settings: theme, low-resource mode, preview and output frame rates, canvas size, the encoders found on your PC, the logs folder and Copy diagnostics.
  • Coming next: capturing displays, windows and cameras, with a live preview and moving and resizing sources on it.
